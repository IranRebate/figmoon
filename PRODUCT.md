# Figmoon product and brand

## Positioning

**Figmoon is a local design-intelligence workspace that gives AI agents complete,
precise, and governed access to Figma without making users switch between tools.**

Primary tagline: **One local workspace from design context to verified code.**

Short description: Figmoon connects Figma to AI-assisted development through
whole-file discovery, design-system context, secure reference evidence, and
conflict-safe operations in one persistent workspace.

## Product pillars

1. **Complete context** — search and inspect across pages, files, editors,
   libraries, variables, prototypes, annotations, accessibility, and exports.
2. **Precise targeting** — use durable node IDs, page context, visible copy, or
   selection as an optional shortcut rather than a mandatory workflow.
3. **Structural design intelligence** — turn exact node geometry, styles, text,
   and component context into a fast design-to-browser comparison before raster
   iteration.
4. **Verifiable fidelity** — acquire identity-checked references and connect
   their hashes to pixel/edge measurement, baseline, and human attestation.
5. **Safe coordination** — route multiple files, expose incremental changes,
   and guard authorized mutations with leases, revisions, dry runs, and rollback.
6. **Local control** — keep workspace events and design data local; upload
   nothing through Figmoon itself.

## Audiences

- Product engineers implementing exact design-to-code work.
- Design-system teams exporting reviewable contracts.
- AI-agent operators coordinating parallel inspection and implementation.
- QA and design reviewers who need reproducible visual evidence.

## Messaging hierarchy

Lead with the outcome: one workspace replaces plugin switching and selection-
only context. Follow with the evidence contract and multi-agent safety. Use the
current MCP tool count as supporting proof, never as the main promise.

Preferred terms: **local workspace**, **whole-file discovery**, **structural
pattern index**, **verified reference**, **conflict-safe**, **selection is
optional**, **design evidence**.

Avoid claims such as “official Figma MCP,” “Figma-approved,” “zero risk,” or
“pixel-perfect automatically.” Figmoon supplies context and evidence; repository
policy and human review still govern writes and final parity.

## Visual direction

The current interface uses a restrained dark, moonlit palette, compact cards,
and clear state labels. Preserve a single bright accent for connection/action,
high-contrast status colors, 8-pixel spacing rhythm, and short operational copy.
The product should feel calm and instrument-like rather than decorative.
